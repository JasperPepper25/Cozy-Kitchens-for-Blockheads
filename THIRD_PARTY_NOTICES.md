# Third-party artwork

The 33 cabinet front, side and top PNGs for oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia,
dark oak, mangrove, cherry, bamboo, crimson and warped wood are unmodified
Farmer's Delight artwork by vectorwing, redistributed under the MIT License.

- Project: https://github.com/vectorwing/FarmersDelight
- Source revision: `e854d56d4eea8dc120650d43da0d1472711797ad` (1.20 branch)
- Original paths: `src/main/resources/assets/farmersdelight/textures/block/*_cabinet_{front,side,top}.png`
- Bundled paths: `assets/cozykitchens/textures/block/cabinet/`
- Copyright (c) 2020 vectorwing
- Complete license: `META-INF/licenses/FarmersDelight-MIT.txt` in the mod JAR,
  or `src/main/resources/META-INF/licenses/FarmersDelight-MIT.txt` in source.
- Per-file source paths and SHA-256 hashes: `art/farmers-delight-provenance.json`.

Pale oak's lattice front, framed side panel and bordered top/bottom panel are
original artwork. Their editable sources are `art/pale_oak_cabinet_front.svg`,
`art/pale_oak_cabinet_side.svg` and `art/pale_oak_cabinet_top.svg`;
`tools/create_pale_cabinet.py` exports the three PNGs.
They and the add-on code and geometry use the root MIT License, copyright
(c) 2026 Rafe Robichaud.

Minecraft textures are referenced at runtime and are not bundled. Cooking for
Blockheads, Balm, Vanilla Backport, Cozy Home and Cluttered remain separate mods;
their code, models and JARs are not redistributed in this release. Farmer's
Delight does not need to be installed for the bundled cabinet artwork to work.
This independent add-on is not an official release by those mods' authors.
